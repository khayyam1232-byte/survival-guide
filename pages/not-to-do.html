What Not To Do

  1. do not try to hunt for food.
    • eat boiled snails/crickets/mantis because a normal person with no gear cannot hunt.

  2. do not drink urine.
    • urine is all the waste products from your body and drinking it can be harmful as you are drinking all the waste your body has filtered out.

  3. do not search for food first.
    • you should find a water source before looking for food.

  4. do not eat any plant.
    • many plants are poisonous and can be fatal if consumed.

  5. do not move around unnecessarily.
    • moving away from your starting point can make it hard for rescuers to find you.
