What To Do To Survive:

  1. Find a clean water source.
    • Without water a person can only survive 3 days.

  2. Build a shelter.
    • Shelter is essential for protection from the weather.

  3. Signal for help using fire smoke.
    • use something to cover the fire and create smoke.

  4. Stay close to the location where you are stranded.
    • Rescuers will likely search the area where you were last seen.

  5. If you find a river, move with the current.
    • People live near rivers.

  6. Urinate about 100m away from your shelter.
    • Urine can attract animals.
